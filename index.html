easyPlug — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

easyPlug is a companion app for electric vehicle drivers. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does with your information.

Data we collect: none

easyPlug does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information. It does not create accounts, show ads, use analytics, or include any crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

Device permissions

The app displays information on the car screen through Android Auto. It requests no sensitive device permissions — no location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or Bluetooth access.

Data stored on your device

Only your own app preferences are stored locally on your device. They never leave the device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

easyPlug is intended for drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the developer contact details on the easyPlug Google Play listing.
