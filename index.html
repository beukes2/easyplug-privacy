easyPlug — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

easyPlug is an electric-vehicle charging navigation app for South Africa. It shows charging stations near you on the car screen (Android Auto) and can navigate to one. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

No account, no tracking

easyPlug has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporters. Nothing about you is stored on our servers — we do not operate any servers that receive your data.

Location

With your permission, easyPlug uses your device's location to place you on the map and to compute the distance to nearby chargers. This happens on your device. Your location is not stored or shared by easyPlug itself.

Third-party services the app contacts

To draw a real map and to route along roads, the app requests data from:

  • OpenStreetMap tile servers (tile.openstreetmap.org) — map images for the area you are viewing. Like any web request, this reveals the requested map area (and therefore your approximate location) to that service. Tiles are cached on your device.
  • OSRM public demo server (router.project-osrm.org) — when you choose a destination, the app sends the start and destination coordinates to calculate a driving route and ETA.

These requests are made over HTTPS and are subject to those services' own policies. No identifier of you or your device is attached by easyPlug.

Data bundled with the app

The charger list is shipped inside the app. It is derived from Open Charge Map (CC-BY-SA) via ChargePointSA's public API, from OpenStreetMap (ODbL), and from operator listings. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors; tiles licensed ODbL. Routing by OSRM.

Data stored on your device

App preferences and cached map tiles are stored locally and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Children

easyPlug is intended for drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, an updated version will be published at this same address with a new effective date.
